# Financial Statement Analysis: HDFC Asset Management Company Limited

**Company:** HDFC Asset Management Company Limited (HDFC AMC)
**Industry:** Asset Management / Mutual Funds
**Business:** Mutual Fund Management, Portfolio Management and Alternative Investment Funds
**Financial Year Analysed:** FY2025 and FY2026
**Currency:** Indian Rupees (₹ crore, unless otherwise stated)

HDFC Asset Management Company Limited is one of India's prominent mutual fund asset management companies. It manages mutual fund schemes and provides investment management services to investors.

HDFC AMC is relevant to the MutualFundAnalytics project because the project analyzes mutual fund data such as NAV, AUM, SIP inflows, fund-house information, scheme performance, portfolio holdings and investor transactions.

AUM is especially relevant to the MutualFundAnalytics project because the project contains AUM by fund house and uses AUM as an important indicator for analyzing the size and scale of mutual fund businesses.

## 5. Balance Sheet Analysis

A balance sheet provides information about the company's financial position at a particular point in time.

The major components considered in the analysis are:

* Assets
* Liabilities
* Equity
* Cash and investments

For an asset management company such as HDFC AMC, balance sheet analysis helps understand the company's own financial strength and should not be confused with the assets held inside the mutual fund schemes it manages.

The mutual fund schemes have their own assets and liabilities, while HDFC AMC has a separate corporate balance sheet.

## 6. Cash Flow Analysis

Cash flow analysis examines the movement of cash through:

* Operating activities
* Investing activities
* Financing activities

Operating cash flow is particularly useful for understanding whether the company's core business generates cash.

Investing cash flows can include investments or purchases and sales of financial assets and other investments.

Financing cash flows can include dividends, borrowings, share-related transactions and other financing activities.

Cash flow analysis complements the Profit & Loss statement because accounting profit and actual cash generation are not always the same.

## 7. Basic Financial Ratio Analysis

The following ratios are useful when evaluating a listed company:

### Profit Margin

Profit Margin = Net Profit ÷ Revenue × 100

It measures how much profit the company generates from its revenue.

### Return on Equity

ROE = Net Profit ÷ Shareholders' Equity × 100

It measures the return generated relative to shareholders' equity.

### P/E Ratio

P/E = Market Price per Share ÷ Earnings per Share

It compares the company's market price with its earnings per share.

### P/B Ratio

P/B = Market Price per Share ÷ Book Value per Share

It compares the market value of the company with its accounting book value.

### EPS

EPS = Profit Attributable to Equity Shareholders ÷ Number of Outstanding Shares

EPS indicates the earnings attributable to each equity share.

These ratios can be combined with the financial statement analysis to understand profitability, valuation and financial performance.
